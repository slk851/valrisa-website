How this calculation works

The annual return is an effective total return, converted to an equivalent monthly rate. Growth is applied monthly, with fixed contributions at the selected time. Returns stay constant and are reinvested. Distribution yield is not the same as total return.

These are hypothetical scenarios, not forecasts. Taxes, fees, inflation and withdrawals are excluded. Values are rounded for display only. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to Valrisa.
