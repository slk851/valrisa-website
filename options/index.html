Valrisa Options Tracker

A private, local-first macOS application for importing brokerage activity, organizing options strategies, and understanding portfolio income.

Built for clear portfolio reporting

Brokerage imports

Import supported position and transaction CSV files, beginning with Schwab, and reconcile records across multiple accounts.

Options strategies

Review open and realized options activity, multi-leg strategies, contracts, rolls, assignments, and linked shares.

Income reporting

See option income, distributions, transaction costs, weekly and monthly results, and account-level summaries.

Local-first privacy

Your imported brokerage data and app records remain on your Mac under your control.
