FROM CSV TO YOUR iPHONE

Bring your brokerage records with you.

Supported Schwab exports and IBKR Activity Statement CSV files import directly. For other brokers, use a standard Valrisa template.

Get templates and detailed import instructions →

  1. Prepare your file

    Download a transactions or positions template and fill it in using a spreadsheet. Keep the English column headings and save as CSV. No column mapping is needed on iPhone.

  2. Save it in Files

    Use iCloud Drive, Safari Downloads, email → Save to Files, or AirDrop from your Mac. Download cloud-only files before selecting them.

  3. Preview, then import

    Tap Import CSV → Browse, choose the file, review the preview and confirm. Check the results against your brokerage statement.

Good to know before you start.

Does it connect to my brokerage?

No. You import files yourself. The app does not request brokerage credentials, provide live prices or execute trades.

Does my Mac purchase include the iPhone app?

Mac and iPhone access are separate purchases. There is no automatic synchronization between them. This page describes the iPhone app.

Can I import records in another currency?

This version reports in USD. Convert non-USD amounts, prices, fees and cost basis before importing. Keep your original statements and exchange-rate records.

Where are my records stored?

Portfolio records are stored locally on your iPhone. SLK85-Labs does not receive your imported portfolio data. Keep backups somewhere you control.
