Reminders

If reminders do not arrive, check notification permission for Life Planner in iPhone Settings, confirm the plan's reminder time and upcoming date, and try a test alert in the app.

Purchases

For Pro purchase questions, use Restore Purchase with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
