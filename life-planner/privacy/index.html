Valrisa Life Planner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Your plans stay on your iPhone. Life Planner stores the plans you create or select, their schedules and reminder times, your language preference, and related settings locally on your device. SLK85-Labs does not receive your plans or reminder content.

Accounts and tracking

Life Planner does not require a Valrisa account, serve advertising, or use third-party behavioral tracking or analytics.

Notifications

When you enable reminders, the app asks iOS to schedule local notifications on your device. You can manage notification permission in iPhone Settings and edit reminder times in the app.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase and restoration use Apple’s App Store services. SLK85-Labs does not receive your payment-card details.

Support communications

If you contact SLK85-Labs for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, device and app-version details, and a description of the problem. This information is used to respond to your request.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to valrisa-support@slk85.com.
