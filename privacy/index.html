Effective September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Valrisa website, operated by SLK85-Labs, handles information when you visit valrisa.com. Individual Valrisa applications may have their own privacy policies describing their specific data practices.

The Valrisa website currently does not require an account and does not provide forms for submitting personal information directly through the site. If you contact SLK85-Labs through a product support channel, information you provide may be used to respond to your request.

The website is delivered using third-party hosting and internet infrastructure. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, and similar data as part of operating, securing, and delivering the website, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

Valrisa does not currently use its own advertising cookies or behavioral advertising on this website. If analytics or additional services are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to describe those practices.

Valrisa applications

Product-specific data practices can differ from the website. Please refer to the privacy policy provided for the applicable Valrisa application. For example, Valrisa PackSimple has a separate product privacy policy.

Children

The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and Valrisa products evolve. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to SLK85-Labs through the support information provided on the relevant Valrisa product page or App Store listing.
