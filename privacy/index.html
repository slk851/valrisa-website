Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Valrisa website and Valrisa Options Tracker, provided by SLK85-Labs, handle information.

Valrisa Options Tracker

Local-first by design. Valrisa Options Tracker stores imported brokerage information and app records locally on your Mac. SLK85-Labs does not receive, collect, or store your portfolio, transaction, position, journal, or imported CSV data.

Information you import or create

Brokerage files, mapped fields, positions, transactions, strategies, dividends, account information, journal entries, and related records remain on your device. You control any files you import, export, back up, restore, or delete.

Accounts and tracking

Valrisa Options Tracker does not require a Valrisa account. SLK85-Labs does not use the app to serve advertising and does not use third-party behavioral tracking or analytics.

Purchases

App purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple through the App Store. SLK85-Labs does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and device information under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Support communications

If you contact SLK85-Labs for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, device and app-version details, and a description of the problem. This information is used to respond to your request. Please do not send brokerage statements, account numbers, passwords, or other sensitive financial information.

Website information

The Valrisa website does not require an account and does not use its own advertising cookies or behavioral advertising. GitHub Pages and other internet infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs when operating and securing the site, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Other Valrisa products

Other Valrisa applications may have product-specific data practices. Valrisa PackSimple has a separate privacy policy.

Children

Valrisa Options Tracker and this website are not directed to children, and SLK85-Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website and Valrisa products evolve. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to valrisa-support@slk85.com.
