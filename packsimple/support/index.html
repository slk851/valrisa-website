Support

Help and information for Valrisa PackSimple.

Getting started

PackSimple helps you create and manage simple packing lists for your trips. Create a trip, add the items you need, and check them off as you pack.

Purchases

If you purchase a PackSimple upgrade through the App Store, the transaction is handled by Apple. If an existing purchase is not recognized, use the restore-purchase option in the app while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Having a problem?

When requesting support, please include your device model, iOS version, PackSimple version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Support contact
For support, please contact SLK85-Labs through the support contact provided with the App Store listing.
